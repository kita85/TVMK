Welcome to the Tennessee Valley Machine Knitters

We are a friendly club for anyone who loves machine knitting. We share ideas, learn new techniques, help each other with knitting problems, and show off what we make.

What we do

We help each other make the most of our machines, share where to find good materials, and knit for our community.

Learn something new

Programs at our meetings and a seminar with a guest demonstrator.

Get help with problems

Bring a tricky project to Trouble-Shooting Tuesday and we'll work it out together.

Give back

We knit for charity and community projects.

Thinking about joining?

You don't need to own a machine. Come to up to two meetings first and see if the club is right for you.
