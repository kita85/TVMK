Our meetings

Anyone who enjoys machine knitting is welcome. You don't need to own a machine, and you can visit before you join.

When

First Tuesday of every month

Want to visit?

Send us an email and we'll tell you the time and place and answer any questions about your first visit.

Email us: {{ site.email }}

Through the year

Every month Our regular meeting on the first Tuesday, with a program and show-and-tell.
Some Tuesdays Trouble-Shooting Tuesday: bring a knitting problem and we'll work it out together. These are optional.
April Our anniversary celebration and installation of new officers.
December Our holiday luncheon.

How to join

  1. 1 Visit up to two meetings.
    At least one should be a regular monthly meeting.
  2. 2 Join as an Active member.
    Pay your dues and fill in a short bio sheet so we can get to know you.
  3. 3 Take part.
    Active members help with programs and keep their details up to date.

Moving away from East Tennessee?

Former Active members who live elsewhere can stay on as Associate members and keep getting the newsletter and club news.
